\subsubsection{Data Sources}
% Describe all data sources used in your project
% Be specific about where each type of data comes from

We collect our data from various authoritative and reputable sources to ensure the factual correctness of our model,
among which are textbooks, journals, and fact-sheets from worldwide institution.

For textbooks, we collect top-tier ones in the field, including 
\textit{Manson's Tropical Infectious Diseases} (23rd edition)~\cite{farrar2014manson}, 
\textit{Manson's Tropical Diseases} (24th edition)~\cite{farrar2023manson},
\textit{Tropical and Parasitic Infections in the Intensive Care Unit}~\cite{feldman2005tropical} 
(as part of the Perspectives on Critical Care Infectious Diseases Series),
and \textit{Current Topics in Tropical Medicine}~\cite{rodriguez2012current}. 
We also crawl the fact-sheets from \ac{WHO}'s official website. 
For the QA-set, we utilize the medbullet\_op5 set from LangAGI-Lab on HuggingFace, 
which contains sampled questions from \ac{USMLE}, a well-known medical licensing examination in the United States.
We also synthesize another set based on 93 clinical case reports provided by the lecturer and 
80 open-access case reports from the \ac{AJTMH}.
The data sources in our collection are summarized in Table~\ref{tab:data-sources}.

\subsubsection{Data Partition}
% Explain how you split the data for different purposes

We partition the collected data according to their conventional use. For \ac{RAG}, we use the textbooks and the fact-sheets to
build the knowledge base. Meanwhile, we utilize the medbullet\_op5 to perform instruction fine-tuning on our model.
Finally, for benchmarking, we synthesize another set from the 173 case reports collected from multiple sources. 
Our partition is visualized in Figure~\ref{fig:data-splits}.

\subsubsection{Data Extraction}
% Describe how you extracted and processed data from each source

We extract the data from its origin and form to further process it, as discussed in the next sections. 
Specifically, we directly download the textbooks from the corresponding official websites or digital libraries,
as well as the clinical case reports from the journal. 
For the fact-sheets from \ac{WHO}, we scrape the HTML content using web scraping tools. 
We obtain the QA-set from HuggingFace.
