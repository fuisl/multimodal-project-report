\subsection{Motivation}
% Describe the motivation behind the project
% Why is multimodal learning important for tropical and infectious disease diagnosis?
% What are the current challenges in clinical decision support systems?

The diagnosis process in the medical context is challenging due to its complexity and variability,
together with the need for fast but accurate decision. 
Previous approaches rely heavily on mono-modal data, which is unrealistic and incomplete, given the nature of 
diagnostic decision-making process. 
Furthermore, existing models are trained on knowledge-heavy data~\cite{thapa2025disentanglingreasoningknowledgemedical},
limiting their reasoning ability, thus undermining their reliability. 
Therefore, we seek to integrate multi-modal capability into our model, as well as improving its reasoning ability
for greater interpretability and reliability.

\subsection{Problem Statement}
% Clearly define the problem you are addressing
% What gaps exist in current approaches?
% What specific challenges do you aim to solve?

Despite advances in medical AI, existing clinical decision support systems face several limitations:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Limited multimodal integration:} Current systems often process text, images, and structured data separately, missing critical cross-modal relationships
    \item \textbf{Lack of contextual understanding:} Traditional models struggle to incorporate patient history, clinical guidelines, and domain-specific knowledge effectively
    \item \textbf{Generalization challenges:} Models trained on limited datasets often fail to generalize across diverse patient populations and disease presentations
    \item \textbf{Interpretability concerns:} Black-box models provide limited insight into diagnostic reasoning, hindering clinical adoption
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Research Objectives}
% List your main objectives clearly
% What do you aim to achieve with your system?

This work aims to address these challenges through the following objectives:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Develop a multimodal diagnostic system} that integrates clinical text, medical images, and structured patient data using large multimodal models (LMMs)
    
    \item \textbf{Implement and compare multiple approaches} including Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), model fine-tuning, and hybrid combinations to determine optimal architectures
    
    \item \textbf{Establish comprehensive evaluation frameworks} with clinically relevant metrics to assess diagnostic accuracy, reasoning quality, and system reliability
    
    \item \textbf{Analyze model performance} across different disease categories and data modalities to identify strengths, limitations, and areas for improvement
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Research Hypothesis}
% State your hypotheses clearly
% What do you expect to find or achieve?

We hypothesize that:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{H1:} Large multimodal models enhanced with domain-specific retrieval mechanisms (RAG) will outperform standalone fine-tuned models in diagnostic accuracy for rare and complex tropical diseases
    
    \item \textbf{H2:} A hybrid approach combining RAG with targeted fine-tuning will achieve superior performance compared to either method alone, particularly in scenarios requiring both broad medical knowledge and disease-specific expertise
    
    \item \textbf{H3:} Models trained on diverse multimodal data sources will demonstrate better generalization across different clinical presentation patterns compared to single-modality approaches
\end{itemize}
